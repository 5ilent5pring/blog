Director of National Intelligence Tulsi Gabbard has appointed Dave Mastro and James Cangialosi to lead a centralized interagency effort to monitor the 2026 election cycle. This consolidation follows the scaling back of the Foreign Malign Influence Center and significant personnel reductions within CISA. The centralization of intelligence oversight within the ODNI, paired with a weakened CISA, creates a structural bottleneck that prevents actionable threat intelligence from reaching the local election officials responsible for physical and digital infrastructure security.
