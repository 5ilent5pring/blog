On May 15, Ukrainian drone strikes hit the Ryazan oil refinery, causing a significant fire and resulting in four fatalities, including a child. This operation marks a departure from previous strikes on the same facility, which primarily targeted industrial infrastructure without causing significant loss of life. The transition from purely industrial sabotage to strikes causing civilian casualties in residential areas suggests a deliberate expansion of the operational envelope to degrade Russian domestic stability and logistics.
