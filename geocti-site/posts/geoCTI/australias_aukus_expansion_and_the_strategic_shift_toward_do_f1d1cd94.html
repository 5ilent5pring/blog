The Australian government has committed $45.2 billion USD to its FY2026-2027 defense budget, prioritizing the AUKUS nuclear submarine program and long-range strike capabilities. This fiscal pivot moves Australia away from a reliance on imported finished platforms toward a high-cost, domestic industrial base for precision munitions and hypersonic weapons. The massive scale of this investment, specifically the $26 billion to $36 billion allocated to the Guided Weapons and Explosive Ordnance Enterprise, indicates that Canberra is attempting to mitigate the risk of supply chain interdiction by building a self-sustaining, localized lethality loop.
