Hackers targeting the Canvas learning management system have claimed to delete stolen student data, a move security professionals view with extreme skepticism. While the attackers use derogatory language to frame themselves as mere 'criminals and scumbags,' the persistence of stolen datasets in underground markets suggests a different operational reality. The attackers' primary objective is likely the long-term monetization of student identities rather than the immediate destruction of the evidence.
