US cybersecurity officials are moving to compress federal vulnerability remediation timelines from 14 days to 72 hours. This policy shift responds to the emergence of specialized LLM-based tools like Anthropic’s Mythos and OpenAI’s GPT-5.4-Cyber, which automate the exploitation of software flaws. The compression of this window forces a fundamental shift in federal IT operations from reactive patching to a continuous, automated deployment model to prevent attackers from outpasting defensive response capabilities.
